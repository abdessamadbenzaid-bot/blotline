## نصوص المتجر

### English (en-US)

**App name**

```
Blotline: Hide Faces in Video
```

**Short description**

```
Blur faces, plates and numbers in videos. Offline, no account, no watermark.
```

**Full description**

```
Share the video. Not the details.

Blotline hides faces, license plates, phone numbers and any other private detail in your videos, right on your phone. Your video is never uploaded: the app does not even request internet access.

HIDE ANYTHING
• Draw a mask directly on the video
• Choose Blackout, Mosaic or Blur
• Use box or oval masks, as many as you need

FOLLOW THE ACTION
• Tracking moves the mask with your subject
• Unsure moments are marked for you to review

LEAK CHECK
Before you export, Blotline lists what deserves a second look: masks that do not cover the whole clip, uncertain tracking, and readable text that needs a stronger mask.

FINISH THE CLIP
• Trim the start and the end
• Keep the sound, remove it, or bleep a name or number
• Export for vertical, square, 4:5 or wide formats
• Save an MP4 up to 1080p with no watermark
• Location and camera data are removed from the exported file

No account. No ads. No analytics.

Blur and mosaic reduce detail but may not hide everything. Use Blackout for numbers and text, and watch the result before sharing.
```
